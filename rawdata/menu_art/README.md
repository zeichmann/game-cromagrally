# High-resolution main menu

The built-in ImageGen tool generated the 1672 × 941 sandstone artwork in
`Data/Images/MainMenuBackgroundHD.png`. The game draws the title, version,
and menu entries over the image. `CMR_ORIGINAL_TEXTURES=1` selects the original
menu artwork for comparison.

## ImageGen prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create original wide 16:9 landscape background artwork at the highest available resolution for a prehistoric desert rally game's main menu. Full-bleed frontal view of one aged dark honey-brown sandstone wall, with realistic fine mineral grains and old chisel marks. Frame with irregular broken strata and natural rocky relief near the far left and right edges. At the lower left add a discreet small carved spiral fossil and stone chips; at the lower right add narrow green desert leaves and weathered little sandstone scree. Reserve the entire central 62% width and the upper middle for separately drawn live text. This space should be calm, dark brown and gently textured, with even lighting and a subtle broad warmer glow below the center. The scene should be tactile, dark rust and dark burnt umber with restrained amber accents and high-quality sharply readable surface detail. Cropping the sides must leave a complete composition. Flat, straight-on, top-to-bottom stone surface with no sky or landscape vista. No visually raised central panel, artificial graphic borders, lettering, symbols that look like buttons, logos, title, UI, watermarks, characters or vehicles.
